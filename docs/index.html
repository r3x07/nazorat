Privacy Policy: Nazorat

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Nazorat does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server. There is no server.

What the app does

Nazorat finds sensitive information in text, such as passwords, payment cards, email addresses, phone numbers and identifiers, and replaces it before you send that text somewhere else. All of this happens on your device: iPhone, iPad or Mac.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, no account, and no login. We cannot see what you type, what you scan, or that you use the app at all.

Network access

The app contains no networking code. You can verify this: turn on Airplane Mode and every feature still works.

The Nazorat keyboard never requests Full Access. Without Full Access, iOS makes network access impossible for a keyboard extension. This is enforced by the operating system, not by our promise.

Nazorat for Mac has no network entitlement, so the macOS sandbox blocks every connection it could try to make.

What stays on your iPhone or iPad

Three things are stored locally and never leave your device:

  • Your settings: which categories to look for, keyboard preferences. Kept in an app group container shared between the app and its keyboard.
  • Your list: values you ask Nazorat to always catch. Kept in the iOS Keychain, marked as device-only, so it never appears in a backup and never syncs to another device.
  • Your history: what was found and what it was replaced with, so you can review it. Also kept in the Keychain, device-only, capped at 200 entries, and erasable at any time in Settings, under History.

Deleting the app removes your settings straight away. iOS keeps Keychain items after an app is deleted, so your list and your history stay on the device, readable by no other app, until Nazorat is installed again: the first time it opens, it erases them. To erase them sooner, clear History and Your list in Settings before deleting the app.

The real values needed to restore an assistant's reply are kept in memory only, and are gone when you start over or close the app.

What the keyboard sees

While the Nazorat keyboard is active it can read the text in the field you are typing into, in order to detect sensitive values. That text is examined on the device and is never stored except as described above, never transmitted, and never shared.

On the Mac

Nazorat for Mac works on the clipboard. It reads what you copied only when you ask it to (with a shortcut, from its menu, or through Services), or after each new copy if you turn on automatic cleaning. Copies that password managers mark as concealed are skipped.

When "Clean when I switch to an assistant" is on, Nazorat checks which app is in front, to know when the ChatGPT or Claude app is. It reads nothing from those apps.

The real values needed to restore a reply are kept in memory only, and are gone when you quit. Your settings and your list are stored in the app's sandbox container on your Mac and never leave it.

Purchases

Payments and subscriptions are handled by Apple, and you manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account settings. We never see your payment details or who you are. Whether you have Nazorat Pro, and how many of today's free cleans you have used, is recorded on your device.

Children

Nazorat is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
