# Privacy Policy — Nazorat

**Last updated: 18 September 2026**

Nazorat does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server.
There is no server.

## What the app does

Nazorat finds sensitive information in text — passwords, payment cards, email
addresses, phone numbers, identifiers — and replaces it before you send that
text somewhere else. All of this happens on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier,
no account, and no login. We cannot see what you type, what you scan, or that
you use the app at all.

## Network access

The app contains no networking code. You can verify this: turn on Airplane Mode
and every feature still works.

The Nazorat keyboard never requests Full Access. Without Full Access, iOS makes
network access impossible for a keyboard extension — this is enforced by the
operating system, not by our promise.

## What stays on your device

Three things are stored locally and never leave your phone:

- **Your settings** — which categories to look for, keyboard preferences. Kept
  in an app group container shared between the app and its keyboard.
- **Your list** — values you ask Nazorat to always catch. Kept in the iOS
  Keychain, marked as device-only, so it never appears in a backup and never
  syncs to another device.
- **Your history** — what was found and what it was replaced with, so you can
  review it. Also kept in the Keychain, device-only, capped at 200 entries, and
  erasable at any time from Settings.

Deleting the app removes all three.

## What the keyboard sees

While the Nazorat keyboard is active it can read the text in the field you are
typing into, in order to detect sensitive values. That text is examined on the
device and is never stored except as described above, never transmitted, and
never shared.

## Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Whether you
have purchased is recorded as a single flag on your device.

## Children

Nazorat is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
